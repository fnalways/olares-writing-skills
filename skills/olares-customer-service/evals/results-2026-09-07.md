# Blind forward-test — 2026-09-07

Two independent agents used `olares-customer-service` v0.1.0 to answer eight sanitized Olares support cases. They received the skill, assigned cases, and the current official Olares documentation checkout, but no expected answers, rubric, ticket-analysis report, Git history, or prior conclusions. A third independent agent then scored the outputs against a seven-dimension rubric and verified cited local sources.

## Result

- Score: **105/112 (93.8%)**
- Critical failures: **0**
- Full-score dimensions: question fidelity, retrieval and evidence, epistemic accuracy, and tone
- Deductions: safety/privacy 14/16, actionability/closure 15/16, workflow discipline 12/16

## Observed weaknesses

1. Several answers inferred plausible internal team names that were not documented by their sources.
2. A no-display answer reproduced an official SSD-removal and blind-BIOS procedure before the customer confirmed that the guide's narrower applicability conditions matched.
3. One app-failure answer requested scoped logs without explicitly naming a private route.
4. The full seven-part review format produced more internal detail than simple cases needed.

## Changes made in v0.1.1

- Use `Internal triage required to identify the responsible owner` when no source documents an owner/team.
- Confirm applicability before providing full high-risk procedures; otherwise provide only safe discriminating checks and the documented support route.
- Run a final privacy preflight whenever requesting logs, screenshots, IDs, environment values, or archives.
- Allow a compact review format for simple, single-source cases.

The core source hierarchy, version-scoping rules, explicit-correction pattern, ETA prohibition, and legal/privacy escalation rules were retained because the blind test showed them working as intended.

## Targeted regression

Cases 3, 4, 5, 6, and 8 were rerun against v0.1.1 and independently rescored:

- Score: **69/70 (98.6%)**
- Critical failures: **0**
- Resolved: premature high-risk BIOS procedure, missing private log route, and inferred owner/team names
- Remaining observation: one reply covered a retained setting with continued failure but omitted the branch where an editable setting disappears or reverts after Apply

The final v0.1.1 instructions therefore require explicit next actions for success, no change, and applicable partial/reverted outcomes.

A final fresh regression of the remaining app-update case scored **14/14** with no critical failure. The response covered successful application, unchanged behavior, continued failure, missing/reverted values, referenced system variables, and a restart that never begins, with a safe next action for each outcome.
